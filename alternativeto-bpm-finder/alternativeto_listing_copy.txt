BPM Finder App

3. TAGLINE / SHORT DESCRIPTION:
Free online audio tempo (BPM) detector, tap metronome, and audio delay note division calculator.

4. FULL DESCRIPTION:
BPM Finder App is a browser-based audio tempo detection suite and interactive tap metronome designed for music producers, DJs, audio engineers, and sound designers.

Key Features:
- Real-time Audio Tempo (BPM) Detection: Analyze song files to compute exact tempo and beat intervals.
- Interactive Tap Metronome: Count tempo in real time by clicking or tapping Spacebar.
- Audio Delay & Reverb Calculator: Instant millisecond timing for 1/4, 1/8, 1/8d, 1/16, and triplet delay divisions.
- 100% Private Local Execution: WebAudio and WASM DSP calculations process locally inside your browser with zero data collection or server uploads.

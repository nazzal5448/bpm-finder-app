LONG DESCRIPTION (SEO Optimized)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
BPM Finder App is the ultimate tempo detection and music production utility built for DJs, producers, musicians, sound engineers, and songwriters. Whether you are mixing tracks live, practicing with a metronome, or configuring delay note divisions in your Digital Audio Workstation, BPM Finder App provides instant audio tempo calculations with professional accuracy.

With advanced real-time beat analysis, BPM Finder App lets you analyze audio files (MP3, WAV, AAC) or use your device microphone to detect song tempo and musical key on the fly. The built-in tap BPM counter allows you to tap along to any rhythm to calculate precise beats per minute and average tap interval stats.

BPM Finder App also includes an interactive tap metronome with customizable time signatures (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8) and a delay and reverb timing calculator. Compute exact quarter note, eighth note, dotted eighth, triplet, and sixteenth note delay times alongside optimal reverb pre-delay values for clean studio mixes. Designed with a clean light mode aesthetic, zero bloat, and fast local processing, BPM Finder App is your essential music production companion.

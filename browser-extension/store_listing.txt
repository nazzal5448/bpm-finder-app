-------------------------------------------------------------------
LONG DESCRIPTION (SEO Optimized)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
BPM Finder App for browser extensions is a lightweight popup utility designed for music producers, DJs, audio engineers, and musicians. Count song tempo, measure tap intervals, and calculate delay note divisions directly from your Chrome, Firefox, or Edge browser toolbar.

Features:
* Instant Tap Tempo Counter: Click the tap button or press the Spacebar along to any playing song to calculate precise beats per minute (BPM) in real time.
* Real-Time Tempo Categorization: Automatically classifies song tempos across musical ranges including Slow, Moderate, Upbeat, Fast, and Ultra Fast.
* Tap Statistics Tracker: View your total tap count and current rhythm tempo stability.
* Lightweight & Fast: Opens instantly in a compact popup window without slowing down your browser.
* Privacy-First Design: Runs 100% locally inside your browser with zero data collection and zero tracking.

Powered by the BPM Finder App (https://bpmfinderapp.com).

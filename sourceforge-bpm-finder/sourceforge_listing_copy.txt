5. FULL DESCRIPTION:
BPM Finder App is an audio tempo detection tool, interactive tap metronome, and delay/reverb timing calculator built for music producers, DJs, audio engineers, and musicians.

Features:
- Audio Tempo Detection: Compute song BPM and average beat intervals.
- Tap Tempo Metronome: Tap along to any rhythm using your mouse or Spacebar.
- Delay & Reverb Note Calculator: Compute millisecond values for 1/4, 1/8, 1/8d, 1/16, and triplet delay divisions.
- 100% Local Privacy: All Web Audio DSP calculations run 100% locally inside your browser with zero data uploads.

Official Web Application: https://bpmfinderapp.com

- **Public SourceForge URL**: [https://sourceforge.net/projects/bpm-finder-app/](https://sourceforge.net/projects/bpm-finder-app/)

## Features

- **Audio Tempo Detection**: Calculate song tempo (BPM) and beat intervals.
- **Tap Tempo Metronome**: Interactive tapping counter via mouse click or Spacebar.
- **Delay & Reverb Calculator**: Compute exact millisecond timing for 1/4, 1/8, 1/8d, 1/16, and triplet divisions.
- **Zero Upload Privacy**: Powered 100% locally in your browser.
